Local Initiatives in Chittoor

NTR Vaidya Seva Scheme

This health initiative provides cashless medical treatment to economically disadvantaged families, reducing the financial burden of healthcare.

Self-Help Groups (SHGs)

SHGs empower women by encouraging them to save, pool resources, and access micro-loans, helping them to become financially independent.

Mango Festival

This annual festival promotes local mango varieties, boosts tourism, and provides market opportunities for farmers.

Integrated Tribal Development Programs

These programs uplift tribal communities through education, health services, and sustainable livelihood opportunities.

Digital Literacy Programs

Workshops enhance digital skills among youth and women, enabling them to access online resources and job opportunities.

Clean and Green Chittoor

This initiative promotes environmental sustainability through community participation in cleanliness drives and tree planting.

Rural Livelihood Mission

This program reduces poverty by promoting self-reliance through skill training and financial support for rural communities.
